Hardware: TITAN RTX / sm_75. FP16 is the controlled compute dtype. Native BF16, FP8 and FlashAttention assumptions are not imported from Ampere/Hopper examples. The [official bitsandbytes support matrix](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/v4.57.0/quantization/bitsandbytes) lists Turing support for LLM.int8 and Pascal-or-newer NF4. Installed binary/kernel compatibility still requires an actual smoke; hardware support is not proof that this environment works. Every result retains quantization, prompt length, generated length, allocated/reserved peaks and latency.

Research motivation: [SANE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00658) studies compact structured evidence consumption, directly motivating a shared textual interface. [SARA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05633) studies textual/compressed representations with model-specific components; its cross-family experiments do not make our existing projector portable. [ChainRAG](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1089/) reports multi-hop retrieval experiments including Qwen2.5-72B, supporting family relevance but not validating this 7B candidate. These are source-reported contexts, not performance claims about our unrun models. No additional obscure candidates are added.

Selection will use only a frozen paper-grouped FIT subset. Canonical Mistral plus at most two alternatives may be frozen. Fixed qualitative samples must be reviewed for unsupported answers before freezing because deterministic quote support does not establish answer entailment. Confirmation splits never select models. No screen or model panel was completed during the patch.
